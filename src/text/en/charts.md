## explained.lead

<!-- "What the numbers mean", on both results pages and in the PDF. The titles are in charts.ts. -->

There's no right or wrong result, and no good or bad score. Each number describes how one run went,
on one screen, on one day. It's just for fun.

## explained.threshold

In both tasks, only some of the dots were organised and the rest were random. After correct answers
the test lowered the share of organised dots, and after a mistake it raised it again, until it
settled where you were right about four times in five. That share is your threshold. A 10%
threshold means that with 10 dots in every 100 organised, you picked the right answer about four
times in five.

## explained.motionAndForm

The motion threshold is how much of the dots had to drift together before you could tell which way
they went. The form threshold is how much had to line up into the starburst before you could tell
which flash held it. They're two different kinds of seeing, so the two numbers don't have to match.

## explained.sensitivity

Sensitivity turns the threshold around: it's 1 ÷ the threshold in percent. A 10% threshold is a
sensitivity of 0.1, and a 5% threshold is 0.2. A smaller threshold and a taller sensitivity bar say
the same thing: less organised signal was needed to pick out the pattern.

## explained.diff

Your motion sensitivity minus your form sensitivity. Above zero, you needed less organised signal to
see the moving dots than the starburst. Below zero, the starburst needed less. Near zero, about the
same for both. It compares your two tasks with each other, and it isn't a score.

## explained.groupBars

<!-- Where no threshold charts are shown: the Results by pseudonym page. -->

A group's bar is its median: half the group's results are above it and half below. The line across
the bar covers the middle half, leaving out the top and bottom quarter. They show how spread out
each group's results are. Nothing is concluded from where yours fall.

## explained.groupBarsWithThresholdCharts

<!-- The same, where the threshold charts are shown too: the Results page and the PDF. -->

A group's bar is its median: half the group's results are above it and half below. The line across
the bar covers the middle half, leaving out the top and bottom quarter. On the threshold charts, the
lighter band covers the middle 80%, leaving out the top and bottom tenth. They show how spread out
each group's results are. Nothing is concluded from where yours fall.

## explained.varies

The same person can get quite different numbers on different days. Screens, lighting, distance from
the screen, tiredness and attention all change how a run goes, and a single run has some luck in it.
