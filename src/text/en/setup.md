## motionCaption

**Motion.** Some of the dots drift together, up or down. You say which way.

## formCaption

**Form.** Two quick flashes of flickering dots. In one, some line up into a starburst. You say which
flash.

## previewsNote

Both examples are at 100% coherence. In the test, the share of organised dots falls until it's hard
to tell.

## needTabletStand

<!-- The first item: what to run it on, for a tablet or for anything else. -->

Somewhere to stand the tablet: a stand, or propped against something on a table, turned sideways.
Don't hold it while the tasks run, and don't move it once you've measured the screen.

## needComputer

A laptop or desktop with a keyboard. Large tablets work in a stand; phones won't.

## needCard

A bank card, or any card the same size, to measure your screen.

## needGlasses

Your usual glasses or contact lenses.

## pleaseRead

<!-- Must say plainly that this is just for fun and isn't medical advice. -->

**Please read.** This is just for fun and isn't medical advice. The tasks show moving and flickering
dots: don't run them if you have photosensitive epilepsy, and press Esc to stop if they make you
feel unwell. If you chose to contribute, your results are sent anonymously when you finish.
Otherwise they stay in this browser unless you save or copy them.

## fullOption

<!-- The two types of test to choose from. {minutes} is how long it takes. -->

**Full test**, about {minutes} minutes. Each task runs as long as in the published studies it's
based on.

## quickOption

<!-- Must say plainly that quick results are less precise, without medical wording. -->

**Quick test**, about {minutes} minutes. Each task runs for about half as long, so the results are
less precise. They're only ever compared with other quick-test results.

## needRoom

<!-- In the list of what you need. {minutes} is how long the test takes. -->

About {minutes} minutes in a dim room, sitting about an arm's length (50 cm) from the screen.
