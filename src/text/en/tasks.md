## intro.caption.motion

Example at 100% coherence, drifting up.

## intro.caption.form

Example at 100% coherence: the starburst, flickering as it will in the test.

## recap.motion

Keep your eyes on the red spot. A square of dots appears for a moment: some drift together, up or
down, while the rest jump about. Which way did the drifting dots go?

## recap.form

Keep your eyes on the grey spot. You'll see two quick flashes of dots. In one, some dots line up
into a starburst, pointing out from the centre; the other is random. Which flash had the starburst?

## how.motion

When the dots disappear, press <kbd>↑</kbd> for up or <kbd>↓</kbd> for down, or click the top or
bottom half of the screen.

## how.form

After the second flash, press <kbd>1</kbd> or <kbd>←</kbd> for the first flash, <kbd>2</kbd> or
<kbd>→</kbd> for the second, or click the left or right half of the screen.

## guess.motion

If you can't tell, guess up or down anyway.

## guess.form

If you can't tell, guess 1 or 2 anyway.

## nowTheTest

Now the test. It gets harder until it's hard to tell. That's expected: answer every time, and guess
when you're unsure. An easy one appears every few trials. During the test, press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to
pause.

## practiseAgainPrompt

Not sure what to do yet? Practise again first.

## pause.body

## intro.copy.motion

<!-- The page before the motion task. -->

You'll see a square of dots for a fifth of a second. Some drift together, either up or down; the
rest jump about at random. Your job is to say which way the drifting dots went. Keep your eyes on
the red spot in the middle so you don't miss them.

When the dots disappear, press <kbd>↑</kbd> if they drifted up or <kbd>↓</kbd> if they drifted
down. You can also click or tap the top or bottom half of the screen. If you can't tell, guess up or
down anyway: the test adapts to you.

## intro.copy.form

<!-- The page before the form task. -->

Each trial shows two quick flashes of flickering dots, one after the other. In one flash, some of
the dots line up into a starburst, pointing out from the centre; the other flash is random. Your job
is to say which flash had the starburst. Keep your eyes on the grey spot in the middle.

After the second flash, press <kbd>1</kbd> or <kbd>←</kbd> if the starburst was in the first flash,
or <kbd>2</kbd> or <kbd>→</kbd> if it was in the second. You can also click or tap the left or
right half of the screen. If you can't tell, guess 1 or 2 anyway: the test adapts to you.
