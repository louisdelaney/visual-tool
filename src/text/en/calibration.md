## screen.instructions

Hold a bank card, or any card the same size, flat against the screen over the outline. Move the
slider until the outline is exactly as wide as the card.

## screen.savedSize

The outline is set to the size saved on this browser. Check it still matches your card.

## screen.keepZoom

Don't change the browser zoom after this step, and keep the window on this screen.

## distance.intro

The tasks are specified in degrees of visual angle, so the page needs to know how far your eyes are
from the screen. It works this out from your blind spot, which sits about 13.5° from wherever you
look. Sit centred in front of the screen, as you will for the tasks: the maths assumes it.

## distance.windowNarrowFullScreen

<!-- When the window is narrower than the screen: with full screen available, or without. -->

This window is narrower than your screen. The blind-spot check and the tasks switch to full screen
when they start, because both assume you're sitting centred on what the page draws.

## distance.windowNarrowNoFullScreen

This window is narrower than your screen, and full screen isn't available here. Maximise the window
and sit centred on it: the blind-spot check and the tasks both assume you're centred on what the
page draws.

## distance.tabletNote

The blind-spot check needs a keyboard's space bar. Without one, measure from your eyes to the screen
with a tape measure while you sit as you will for the tasks, and enter it below.

## blindSpot.missed

The dot reached the edge without you pressing <kbd>Space</kbd>. Check your right eye is fully
covered, and keep your left eye on the square rather than the dot. If it still never vanishes, stop
and enter your distance by hand.

## blindSpot.steps

<!-- The blind-spot check's instructions, on the test screen. -->

1. Sit centred in front of the screen, the way you will for the test, about an arm's length away.
2. Cover your **right** eye with your hand.
3. Look at the black square and keep looking at it. Don't follow the dot: you'll still see it out of the corner of your eye.
4. A red dot moves left, vanishes for a second or two, then comes back. Press <kbd>Space</kbd> the moment it vanishes.

## blindSpot.repeats

<!-- Under the steps. {times} is how many readings it takes. -->

This repeats {times} times. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> at any point to stop.
