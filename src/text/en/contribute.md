## measuresIntro

Your visual system handles movement and shape partly along separate routes, so the test has one task
for each. In the motion task, some dots drift together, up or down. In the form task, some line up
into a starburst. Each task finds the smallest share of organised dots you can still pick out. The
smaller that share, the higher your sensitivity for that task.

## measuresCompare

Your results then compare the two. Motion minus form shows which one you picked out more easily, and
by how much. People vary, and the dataset is here to show how much.

## noGoodOrBad

<!-- Must stay neutral: no result is better or worse than another (see AGENTS.md). -->

**There are no good or bad results, and no wrong answers.** Every result is just a number about how
you saw the dots that day.

## usualSelf

The test works best when you're as you usually are: taking any medication you take regularly, and
not under the influence of alcohol or other intoxicants.

## phone.notice

This screen is too small to show the tasks at the size they need from an arm's length away, so the
test won't start here. To take it, open this page on a laptop or desktop computer, or on a large
tablet propped up in a stand.

## phone.others

Everything else works here. You can [see your previous results](history), [download the data](data)
or [read about privacy and the risks](privacy).

## tablet

<!-- Above the contribute form on a tablet. -->

**On a tablet?** You can take the test on one, but only if it stands still the whole time: in a
stand or propped against something on a table, turned sideways, about an arm's length (50 cm) from
your eyes. Don't hold it. The page measures your screen and how far away it is, and a tablet in your
hands moves, so the dots wouldn't be shown at the size they're meant to be. This matters most if you
contribute: please only send results from a tablet that stayed put. A laptop or desktop is easier if
you have one.

## contributeIntro

You can add your results to a public dataset. If you say yes, they're sent with your answers below
when you finish the test. Either way, you'll see your results at the end and can save them as a
file.

## publicAnonymous

<!-- The two bold key points. These are promises made on the Privacy and risks page too. -->

**All data you enter will be public but anonymous.**

## nothingLogged

**Nothing extra is logged.** Only your answers, results and the test's technical details are sent,
and only if you say yes. Anyone can download the data, and no name, email or IP address is kept.

## privacyAndHistory

[Read about privacy and the risks](privacy) before you decide. Contributed before? [See your
previous results](history).

## pseudonymHint

Completely optional. Leave it blank and your results can't be looked up or linked together, which is
the most private choice. If you use one, use the same one each time to see your results over time.
It's public: anyone who enters it can see every result and answer sent with it, so don't use your
name, a username you use elsewhere, or anything else that points to you. A generated one is safest;
copy it somewhere or write it down.

## sinceHint

Your best estimate is fine. If it's been less than a month, enter 0 and 0.

## psychedelicsUsedHint

Add one at a time. Public, like all your answers: no names of people or places.

## medicationsHint

Add the names one at a time. Public, like all your answers: no names of people or places.

## notesHint

For example, a change in medication or symptoms since your last test, if you're tracking over time.
Public: no names, contact details, or anything else that points to you.

## foundViaOtherHint

Public: no names of people or private groups.
